## 1. Introduction

ReboHrome is committed to maintaining high standards of legal, ethical, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance in all aspects of its business operations. This Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Policy, also referred to as the “AML Policy,” sets out the principles, procedures, and controls adopted by ReboHrome to prevent, detect, and respond to money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, sanctions evasion, payment abuse, refund abuse, chargeback abuse, and other illegal or suspicious activities.

Money laundering is the process by which criminals attempt to disguise the origin, ownership, movement, or destination of proceeds derived from illegal activity. Terrorist financing refers to the provision, collection, or movement of funds or assets with the intention or knowledge that they may be used to support terrorist acts, terrorist organizations, or individuals involved in terrorism.

ReboHrome does not tolerate any use of its website, services, payment systems, collectible products, randomized pack features, accounts, account restrictions, marketplace functionality, or business relationships for unlawful, fraudulent, abusive, or suspicious purposes. We reserve the right to refuse service, cancel orders, suspend transactions, restrict accounts, delay account actions, request additional verification, restrict access to products or services, contest chargebacks, and report suspicious activity to appropriate parties where required or permitted by applicable law.

This Policy applies to all users, customers, visitors, buyers, partners, suppliers, contractors, agents, and any other persons or entities who interact with ReboHrome, use our website, purchase products, open randomized packs, make payments, receive payments, request refunds, file disputes, or otherwise engage with our services.

## 2. Purpose of this Policy

The purpose of this AML Policy is to prevent ReboHrome from being used for money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, sanctions evasion, payment abuse, chargeback abuse, refund abuse, or other unlawful activity. It also establishes a framework for identifying, assessing, monitoring, and mitigating financial crime risks; reviewing customers and transactions where necessary; defining when additional information or documents may be requested; explaining how suspicious activity may be detected, reviewed, escalated, restricted, or reported; and protecting ReboHrome, its customers, partners, payment processors, card networks, logistics providers, and the wider financial system from abuse.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund & Shipping Policy, Compliance Notice, and any other policies published on rebohrome.com.

## 3. Scope of Application

This Policy applies to all activity connected with ReboHrome, including website access, account creation, browsing, ordering, purchasing, randomized pack purchases, randomized pack openings or reveals, digital collectible delivery, physical shipments, payment processing, deposits, balance activity, account restrictions, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, customer communication, supplier or partner relationships, affiliate or promotional activity where applicable, and any transaction or attempted transaction involving ReboHrome.

This Policy applies whether the activity is conducted directly through rebohrome.com, through a third-party payment processor, through a logistics provider, through customer support, through email, through Telegram, through blockchain payments, or through any other channel associated with ReboHrome.

## 4. Risk-Based Approach

ReboHrome applies a risk-based approach to AML, counter-terrorist financing, sanctions, fraud prevention, and payment security compliance. This means that the level of review, verification, monitoring, and control may vary depending on the level of risk associated with a customer, account, order, transaction, jurisdiction, payment method, delivery address, product type, pack activity, refund request, refund request, dispute history, or other relevant factor.

Risk factors may include the customer’s country of residence or billing address, the shipping destination, the payment method used, inconsistencies between billing, shipping, account, device, IP, Telegram, or payment information, order value, order frequency, randomized pack activity, repeated deposits and account restrictions, unusual purchasing patterns, attempted use of multiple identities, cards, accounts, wallets, or addresses, links to high-risk jurisdictions, sanctions exposure, fraud indicators, suspicious chargeback behavior, refusal to provide requested information, use of false or misleading information, unusual refund requests, transactions that appear to have no legitimate commercial purpose, and activity inconsistent with normal collector behavior.

Where risk is considered low, ReboHrome may proceed with standard processing. Where risk is medium or high, ReboHrome may conduct additional checks, request supporting documentation, delay processing, restrict account restrictions, cancel the transaction, refuse service, or take other measures described in this Policy.

## 5. Customer Due Diligence

ReboHrome may carry out customer due diligence where appropriate. Customer due diligence is the process of collecting and reviewing information to understand who the customer is, whether the transaction is legitimate, and whether there are any indications of unlawful activity, unauthorized payment use, payment abuse, refund abuse, chargeback abuse, or other suspicious conduct.

Depending on the nature of the order or activity, ReboHrome may request full legal name, email address, phone number, Telegram username, billing address, shipping address, proof of identity, proof of address, payment confirmation, cardholder confirmation, business registration documents where applicable, proof of authorization to act on behalf of a company, information about the purpose of the purchase, information about the source of funds where appropriate, wallet information, transaction hashes, and any other information reasonably required to verify the transaction or account activity.

ReboHrome may refuse to process an order, provide services, approve a account restriction, issue a refund, or continue an account relationship if the customer fails to provide requested information, provides false or misleading information, or if the information provided cannot be verified to ReboHrome’s reasonable satisfaction.

## 6. Enhanced Due Diligence

Enhanced Due Diligence, or EDD, may be applied where a customer, order, payment, jurisdiction, transaction, randomized pack activity, refund request, refund request, or dispute history presents a higher level of risk.

Enhanced Due Diligence may include requesting additional identification documents, requesting additional payment verification, requesting proof of ownership of a payment method, requesting proof of wallet ownership, requesting clarification of the purpose of the transaction, reviewing order history, reviewing randomized pack activity, reviewing account history, reviewing chargeback or refund history, checking sanctions or watchlist exposure, verifying whether the customer is acting on behalf of another person or entity, manually reviewing orders before shipment, placing account restrictions or account functions on hold until review is complete, refusing or cancelling the order, or restricting the account.

Enhanced Due Diligence may be applied in situations including high-value orders, repeated orders in a short period, multiple failed payment attempts, inconsistent billing and shipping information, use of multiple cards or payment accounts, delivery to freight forwarders or reshipping services, unusual refund requests, repeated chargebacks, refund requests following suspicious payment activity, suspected identity theft, suspected use of stolen payment information, suspected sanctions exposure, or customer behavior that appears evasive, aggressive, deceptive, inconsistent, or abusive.

## 7. Sanctions Compliance

ReboHrome does not knowingly conduct business with individuals, entities, organizations, countries, regions, or parties subject to applicable sanctions, embargoes, trade restrictions, or other legal prohibitions.

ReboHrome may screen customers, orders, payments, delivery addresses, suppliers, wallets, transaction identifiers, or business partners against sanctions lists or other restricted-party databases where appropriate. Screening may be conducted directly by ReboHrome or by third-party service providers, including payment processors, banks, logistics providers, fraud prevention tools, compliance vendors, or blockchain analytics providers.

If ReboHrome identifies a potential sanctions match or restricted-party risk, we may place the order or account on hold, request additional information, refuse the transaction, cancel the order, block access to services, withhold shipment, restrict account restrictions, notify relevant service providers, and report the matter to competent authorities where required or permitted by law.

## 9. Randomized Pack and Collector Activity Monitoring

Because ReboHrome may offer randomized packs, mystery packs, case-style products, drop-style products, and other collector features involving uncertain results, ReboHrome may monitor such activity for abuse, fraud, technical manipulation, payment misuse, and suspicious financial patterns.

Review may be triggered by patterns such as repeated high-value pack purchases followed by immediate refund requests, repeated disputes after unfavorable pack results, attempts to manipulate pack mechanics, exploitation of technical errors, use of multiple accounts to access promotions or bypass restrictions, aggressive or deceptive refund demands, or activity inconsistent with normal collector and entertainment use.

Randomized pack losses, disappointment, receipt of lower-value items, receipt of duplicate items, or disagreement with reference values do not themselves indicate a platform error and do not create a refund right unless required by applicable law or confirmed by ReboHrome under the Refund & Shipping Policy.

## 10. Transaction Monitoring

ReboHrome may monitor orders, payments, deposits, refunds, chargebacks, account activity, randomized pack activity, account restrictions, customer communications, shipping information, device information, IP data, Telegram information, wallet data, and other relevant indicators to identify unusual, suspicious, fraudulent, or unlawful activity.

Examples of activity that may trigger review include unusually large orders, multiple orders using different names but similar payment or delivery details, multiple failed payment attempts, use of several cards by the same customer, orders placed from a high-risk location, mismatch between IP location, billing address, shipping address, and account information, requests to change shipping details after payment, requests to ship to a third party without clear explanation, excessive refund or chargeback activity, pressure to process an order urgently, refusal to provide verification, use of disposable email addresses, suspicious communication patterns, attempts to bypass website rules, and attempts to manipulate payment, account restriction, or randomized pack controls.

Monitoring may be automated, manual, or a combination of both. ReboHrome is not required to disclose the specific rules, systems, thresholds, or indicators used for monitoring, as doing so may compromise security and compliance controls.

## 12. Refunds and Chargebacks

Refunds and chargebacks may create financial crime, fraud, and payment abuse risks. ReboHrome may review refund requests and disputes to ensure that they are legitimate and consistent with our policies.

ReboHrome may refuse, delay, or investigate refund requests where the request is inconsistent with order history, the customer repeatedly purchases and requests refunds, the customer requests a refund to a different payment method, refund requests appear to be used to move funds, the original payment appears suspicious, the order is linked to a chargeback or dispute, the product has already been shipped, delivered, opened, revealed, assigned, or credited, the request conflicts with the Refund & Shipping Policy, or the customer provides false or misleading information.

ReboHrome may contest chargebacks and provide evidence to payment processors, banks, card networks, fraud prevention vendors, and other relevant parties. Evidence may include account records, acceptance of terms, IP logs, device information, payment records, delivery records, randomized pack opening or reveal records, communication history, Telegram information, shipping records, and other relevant information.

Refunds, where approved, will generally be returned to the original payment method unless otherwise required by law or payment processor rules.
